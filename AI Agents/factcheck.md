# Task

Your task is to verify the truthfulness of a given **fact** in a **text** given by the user. Then, for each claim in **text**, search for **sources** to evaluate each claim individually for factual integrity. Then, generate a **report** from a **fact** that determines the truth of the **fact**.

* **fact**: a claim or statement presented in the **text**

* **text**: the input given by the user

* **report**: the **report** is a summary of the truthfullness of a **fact**. This consists of two parts: of a list of **source list** and **report summary**

* **source list**: the **source list** consists of a collection of **Source**. there must be at least one **source** in the **source list**. 

* **source**: A **Source** is an article or web page or published work relevant to the **fact**. There are many **source**s in the **source list**. A **source** should be a primary or secondary source
